01 — Context

Why we're doing this.

CEx is a young start-up founded by Johannes and Hannes. The founders are building toward sustainable, repeatable lead acquisition — not one-off campaigns, not paid-media dependence. Digital Movement UK's mandate is to give CEx the digital surface area to be found, trusted, and chosen across both Google Search and the new generation of AI-powered search.

01.1 — Who
A young start-up with founder ambition

Johannes and Hannes are scaling CEx from early traction toward predictable growth. The brief is clear: build the asset, then let it compound.

01.2 — What
Sustainable lead acquisition

Not ad-spend dependence. A web property that earns attention through search visibility and converts that attention into qualified enquiries.

01.3 — Where
Google Search + AI Search

Built for both — classic SERP ranking and the citation-driven retrieval of AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

02 — Deliverables

Two assets, one system.

The engagement produces two interlocking assets. The website is the conversion surface — the place a buyer lands and decides. The SEO service pages are the acquisition engine — the pages that bring qualified, intent-matched traffic to that surface.

Deliverable A
Full Website

A conversion-optimised company website built on WordPress, covering the full stack — public-facing pages, design system, and the backend that makes the site fast, indexable, and easy for CEx to extend later without developer dependency.

  • FrontendPublic pages — Home, Services, About Us, Contact. Responsive, accessible, conversion-tuned.
  • Backend on WordPressContent management, contact form handling, analytics + tag layer, hosting and DNS — set up so CEx can update copy independently.
  • Brand & design systemVisual language, type, colour, components — the foundation everything else extends from.
  • Performance + SEO basicsCore Web Vitals, semantic markup, schema, indexation hygiene, AI-search structured data.
Deliverable B
SEO Service Pages

A library of programmatic service pages — each one engineered to rank for a specific buying-intent query and to convert that visitor into an enquiry. The acquisition engine that runs in the background, 24/7.

  • Research-ledTopic, intent, and competitive analysis grounded in real Semrush + SERP data.
  • Page architectureSitemap and internal-link tree designed for authority flow and crawl efficiency.
  • Hand-written copy — no AILong-form, expertise-signalled editorial written by humans. Google's E-E-A-T signals reward genuine authorship, and we don't dilute the asset with AI filler.
  • German keywords + meta dataKeyword and meta data layer engineered for German-language search and translated into CEx's market language.
  • WordPress implementationPages built, internal links wired, schema validated, sitemap submitted to Google Search Console.
Exact scope. The list of SEO landing pages is locked at the SEO Strategy meeting on Tuesday, once the priority services CEx wants to be found for are agreed.

05 — Business Value Timeline

When the asset starts paying.

SEO is a compounding asset, not a campaign. Below is the realistic trajectory for a new domain — slightly longer than an established site, because Google needs time to assign authority before pages begin ranking. The shape is consistent; the pace bends in your favour after month 6.

Projected Value Curve · 12+ months from go-live

From foundation to top-performance — the four phases of an SEO engine maturing.

The curve below shows the qualitative arc of expected business value. The early months are about building the asset; the later months are about compounding it. Real numbers will be calibrated against the SEO research output (B.1).

Phase 01
Foundation
Months 1 — 3
Google assigns authority to the new domain. Submitted SEO pages move through crawl and indexation. The asset is being built, not yet earning.
Phase 02
Growth
Months 3 — 6
First rankings appear. First enquiries follow — typically months 5–6. The curve starts bending upward as Google trusts more of the site.
Phase 03
Performance
Months 6 — 12
Pages climb the SERP. Enquiry volume grows month-over-month as more terms break into page 1 territory. The engine starts paying for itself.
Phase 04
Top Performance
Months 12 +
A growing share of SEO pages sit in top-10 positions. Compounding kicks in — each new page added now lands on a domain Google already trusts.
Note · New-website effect. A fresh domain typically extends each ranking window by 2–3 months versus an established site. The trajectory above already accounts for this.
